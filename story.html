✦✦ ✦✦ 2018

Endeavour

MayankTwo strangers at MBA coaching, arguing over mock tests and staying back after class for no academic reason whatsoever. I told everyone she was just a good friend. Nobody believed me. They were right.
NehaHe says "study partner," I say I did most of the studying while he did most of the talking. But the classes I remember best are the ones where we didn't study at all.
✦✦ ✦✦ The in-between years

Different cities

NehaDifferent cities, same conversation — it just never really ended, only paused. Whatever happened, good or bad, my thumb somehow always found his name first.
MayankLife sent us to different cities, so we became each other's 2 AM phone call. Wins, disasters, everything in between — she heard about it first. Eight years is a long time to not take a hint.
✦✦ ✦✦ Bangalore

Four days

MayankWhen she was moving to Canada, I flew to Bangalore just to spend time with her — four days of cooking, partying, and laughing till 3 AM. I went home having said everything except the one thing that mattered.
NehaHe showed up in Bangalore like it was totally casual. Four days felt like four hours. I boarded my flight wondering why goodbye felt that heavy. (I had a hint. I also wasn't taking it.)
✦✦ ✦✦ September 30, 2025

Delhi

NehaI ran to the station to pick him up and told myself it meant nothing. Then CP, then that evening, and suddenly eight years of "we're just friends" collapsed in about eight seconds. Best collapse of my life.
MayankShe came back to India, and I couldn't get to Delhi fast enough. She picked me up at the station, the auto ride did what eight years of phone calls couldn't, and somewhere in Connaught Place we finally stopped pretending.
✦✦ ✦✦ The proposal

She said yes

MayankI proposed the only way I know how — I built her an entire website. Voice notes, terrible jokes, a NO button that ran away from her cursor. She said yes anyway. (The NO button remains undefeated.)
NehaHe made me a website. With a NO button that literally dodged me — as if I was going to click it. I said yes before the confetti finished falling. Then Japan: we made rings for each other, and by Lake Kawaguchiko with Fuji watching, I said yes all over again.
✦✦ ✦✦ December 2026

Pawna

NehaFrom coaching-class benches to a mountaintop mandap. We eat, we dance, we sit around the bonfire, and nobody drives home. Come be part of the best chapter yet.
MayankEight years, two continents, one very patient love story. Now we're taking over a hilltop by Pawna Lake to marry at sunset — and it wouldn't be complete without you on that hill.
